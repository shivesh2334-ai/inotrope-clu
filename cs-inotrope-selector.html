Scope of evidence. Per the source review: no pharmacological therapy has proven mortality benefit in cardiogenic shock (DOREMI, LIDO, SURVIVE, SOAP-II and the Cochrane meta-analysis were consistently neutral or only hypothesis-generating). This tool encodes a therapeutic algorithm built from a mix of RCTs and observational registries where trial data is absent, and is intended to structure — not replace — bedside decision-making, invasive haemodynamic assessment, and escalation planning to MCS.
Built on Riccardi M, Pagnesi M, Chioncel O, et al. Medical therapy of cardiogenic shock: contemporary use of inotropes and vasopressors.
