What this does not do. No Shapiro-Wilk normality test — the parametric test (Welch's t-test / one-way ANOVA) always runs, without falling back to Mann-Whitney/Kruskal-Wallis for skewed data. Multivariable logistic regression and Kaplan-Meier confidence bands are Python-only (analytics/cs_stats_engine.py). For manuscript-grade analysis, use that CLI instead.
